Terms of use

Last updated September 19, 2026

SoundProof is a demonstration music studio for writing song ideas, building instrumental previews, processing imported audio, and recording vocals. Use it responsibly and keep copies of work you want to retain.

Your music and imported files

You remain responsible for the audio, lyrics, and prompts you bring to SoundProof. Import songs or recordings only when you have the rights or permission needed for your intended use. SoundProof's ability to separate vocals or change key does not grant rights to someone else's music. Do not use the service to impersonate another artist or violate another person's rights.

What the tools provide

Procedural instrumental previews, local vocal reduction, tempo estimates, key changes, and AI-generated words are creative aids. Results can be inaccurate or incomplete, and vocal removal may leave some voice or remove parts of the instruments. Review the result before performing, distributing, or relying on it.

Availability and storage

This is a college-demo project. AI features depend on provider access and quota. The free hosting environment and browser-local storage are not a backup service; accounts, sessions, and locally saved work can become unavailable. Export recordings and instrumental tracks you want to keep.
